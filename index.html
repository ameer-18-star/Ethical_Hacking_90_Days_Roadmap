90 Days of
Ethical Hacking & Cybersecurity

A complete day-by-day roadmap — not just a resource dump. Every stage has a topic, a time estimate, exactly where to learn it for free, exactly where to practice it hands-on, and a tip from someone who has actually done the job in the real world. Covers foundations, Linux & scripting, traffic analysis, web hacking, exploitation, cloud & blue team operations, and the career launch at the end.

Day 1–3
How the Internet Actually Works
⏱ 2–3 hrs/day
›
OSI Model TCP/IP Stack DNS Resolution HTTP/HTTPS IP Addressing
Learn
  • Professor Messer — Network+ videos on OSI & TCP/IP (free)
  • Cloudflare Learning Center — “What happens when you type a URL”
  • MDN Web Docs — HTTP overview
Practice
  • Trace a DNS resolution end to end with dig and nslookup
  • Run traceroute against 3 different sites and diagram every hop
  • Draw the OSI model from memory, then label a real packet capture against it
💡 Pro Tip

“Don't memorize the OSI model as trivia — use it as a debugging checklist. Every networking problem you'll ever troubleshoot lives at one of those seven layers.”

— Network+ instructor

›
Subnetting CIDR Routing NAT VPNs Ports & Protocols
Learn
  • Professor Messer — full Network+ course (free)

Practice

💡 Pro Tip

“Subnetting math feels pointless until the day you're arguing with a firewall rule at 2 a.m. Then it's the only thing that saves you.”

— Network engineer

Learn

Practice

“Every control you'll ever recommend traces back to CIA. If you can't say which leg of the triad a fix protects, you don't understand the fix yet.”

Day 89–90
Career Launch
⏱ 3–4 hrs/day
›
Resume Building LinkedIn Optimization Certifications Roadmap Where to Apply Networking
Learn
  • CompTIA Security+ (~$400) — entry-level, corporate-recognized
  • eJPT by INE/eLearnSecurity ($200) — first hands-on cert
  • PNPT by TCM Security ($400) — realistic, affordable
  • OSCP (~$1,500) — the industry gold standard
Practice
  • Rebuild your resume around the machines and labs from this roadmap, not just “learned X” bullet points
  • Apply to at least 10 roles: LinkedIn, Indeed, and direct applications to MSSPs (Rapid7, Trustwave, NCC Group)
  • Message 5 people already in the field — ask one specific question each, not “can you help me get a job”
💡 Pro Tip

“Don't wait until you feel ‘ready.’ Apply when you've finished this roadmap. Imposter syndrome is universal in this field. The job will teach you faster than any more studying will.”

— CISO, mid-size tech company
